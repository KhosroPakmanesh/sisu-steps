# Partitive pronouns and questions — pedagogy assessment

## Topic, level and scope

Pack `partitive-pronouns-questions`, version 1.0.0; beginner written grammar within `0 - A1.3`. The approved topic contains singular and plural material with independent Focused preparation. All Finnish is standard written Finnish; explanatory prose is English.

## Sources and coverage blueprint

The [shared partitive assessment](partitive-family.md#verified-sources-and-decisions) records verified sources and their scope. The [complete target inventory](partitive-family.md#coverage-blueprint-and-proposed-counts) records every lesson and proposed count before bulk authoring.

Singular/plural personal object pronouns precede singular/plural demonstratives, followed by mitä/ketä selection in familiar object questions. Pronouns belong in grammar sections and grammarBaseForms, not lexical lists.

Person and number cues disambiguate English you. Near, farther and prior-reference cues make demonstrative choices assessable. Häntä permits him, her and him or her in every accepted English frame.

Proposed and authored inventory: 5 lessons and Focused tests, 116 Focused questions, one 20-question cumulative Review, 136 scored questions overall and 15 optional questions. Each lesson has three optional items and at most ten new lexical entries. Prerequisite chains resolve inside this pack; the first target introduces its pattern independently.
