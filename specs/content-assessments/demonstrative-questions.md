# Finnish-teaching pedagogy assessment: demonstrative-questions

## Pack identity and counts

- Pack ID: `demonstrative-questions`
- Topic: bounded `olla` questions with nominative demonstratives in standard written Finnish
- Level: `0 - A1.3`
- Proposed count: 160 scored exercises in six Focused tests of 20, 24, 20, 20, 20, and 24 questions followed by one 32-question Review; 24 optional practice exercises
- Final count: 160 scored exercises and 24 optional practice exercises

## Important-skill coverage and sequence

1. Ask what one referent is with the bounded frame `Mikä tämä on?`.
2. Ask what several referents are with the bounded classification frame `Mitä nämä ovat?`.
3. Build singular affirmative yes/no questions with `Onko ...?`.
4. Build plural affirmative yes/no questions with `Ovatko ...?`.
5. Build singular negative yes/no questions with `Eikö ... ole?`.
6. Build plural negative yes/no questions with `Eivätkö ... ole?`.

The Review combines only these six question types. General `mikä`/`mitä`/`mitkä` selection and short answers are excluded.

## Pre-authoring gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The boundary is coherent as a question-operation pack. Each question family receives its own Focused lesson and test before the mixed Review.
- Demonstrative reference is supplied whenever question construction is the target. `Mitä` is taught only as part of the plural classification frame, not as a general partitive system.
- Likely errors are retaining statement order, omitting `-ko`, using `onko` with plural subjects, using `ovatko` with singular subjects, building `ei oleko`, omitting `ole` after `eikö` or `eivätkö`, and substituting `mitkä` in the bounded classification question.
- Concrete identities and descriptions will be controlled through translated or lesson-owned vocabulary. Every Finnish production prompt will display its complete English meaning.
- The 128 Focused questions weight plural and negative-plural constructions more heavily. The 32-question Review permits all five response formats, spaced retrieval, and same-skill parallel pairs.
- The pack is written-only and excludes open conversation, intonation, listening, pronunciation, general interrogative declension, and overall CEFR certification.

No unresolved high-impact issue remains before authoring.

## Final gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The finished topology is six Focused lessons and tests followed by one Review. The Focused counts are 20, 24, 20, 20, 20, and 24; the Review contains 32 questions and mixes only the six taught skills.
- Target coverage is 26 singular `Mikä ... on?` items, 30 bounded plural `Mitä ... ovat?` items, 24 singular affirmative yes/no items, 24 plural affirmative yes/no items, 26 singular negative yes/no items, and 30 plural negative yes/no items.
- All 160 scored items use complete questions. The response distribution is 32 multiple choice, 36 fill-in, 34 Finnish construction, 30 Finnish-to-English translation, and 28 word order; the 24 optional items use all five formats.
- The vocabulary ledger contains 27 introduced and 39 reused entries with no hidden supplied vocabulary. Every reused entry resolves through the prerequisite chain, and no lesson introduces more than ten words.
- The six Focused validators require `Mikä ... on?`, bounded `Mitä ... ovat?`, `Onko`, `Ovatko`, `Eikö ... ole`, and `Eivätkö ... ole` respectively. Deferred `mitkä`, general interrogative selection, and short answers are not accepted.
- All question translations now use natural English word order, with plain “that/those” for visibly supplied `tuo`/`nuo` noun phrases and previously identified wording for `se`/`ne`. Every supplied plural noun gloss keeps plural English number.
- All 37 multiple-choice items, including optional practice, give each wrong option distinct feedback about question word, statement order, polarity, number agreement, or required `ole`. Every scored item retains its misconception category, complete sentence explanation, and mutual same-skill/same-format different-answer mastery partner; no same-skill Finnish question repeats within one response type.
- Direct-source validation checks the Finnish sentence behind every response type, including Finnish-to-English tasks. Thirteen family unit cases—twelve negative mutations plus the complete-family acceptance case—cover topology, initial form support, Review ownership, scope, language quality, distinctness, feedback, register, question and inessive boundaries, and mastery pairs.
- The complete client gate passes 169 unit and 14 integration tests. Six dedicated browser cases pass across the configured 320-, 768-, and 1440-pixel projects, including all five demonstrative routes, exact topology, responsive catalog cards, and this longest learning map.

## Final decision

The count and split are approved for controlled `0 - A1.3` written question formation. General `mikä`/`mitä`/`mitkä` selection, the partitive system, short answers, spoken Finnish, listening, speaking, free interaction, calibrated difficulty, and overall CEFR ability remain outside scope.
