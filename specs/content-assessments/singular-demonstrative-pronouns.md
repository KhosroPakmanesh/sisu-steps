# Finnish-teaching pedagogy assessment: singular-demonstrative-pronouns

## Pack identity and counts

- Pack ID: `singular-demonstrative-pronouns`
- Topic: nominative singular demonstratives in standard written Finnish
- Level: `0 - A1.3`
- Proposed count: 120 scored exercises in five Focused tests of 20, 24, 16, 20, and 16 questions followed by one 24-question Review; 20 optional practice exercises
- Final count: 120 scored exercises and 20 optional practice exercises

## Important-skill coverage and sequence

1. Recognise and produce `tämä`, `tuo`, and `se`.
2. Choose among a nearby referent, a farther jointly visible referent, and a referent identifiable to the listener or from context.
3. Use a singular demonstrative independently.
4. Use a singular demonstrative before a nominative singular noun.
5. Distinguish standard-written `se` for a non-human referent from `hän` for a person without claiming that `tämä` and `tuo` cannot identify people.

The final Review mixes only these five decisions. General textual-reference nuance, article-like `se`, colloquial person reference, and every non-nominative form are excluded.

## Pre-authoring gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The boundary is coherent and suitable for a first demonstrative pack. Form recognition precedes reference choice, then independent and noun-modifying use, then the standard-register `se`/`hän` contrast.
- Each Focused target asks for one new decision. Complete sentence frames, `olla` forms, and supporting noun forms will be visibly supplied whenever they are not the target.
- Likely errors are translating English “that” mechanically, confusing `tuo` with context-known `se`, inserting an unnecessary noun, omitting the noun after a modifier, and using colloquial `se` for a person in standard writing. Lessons and diagnostics will address each error directly.
- Teaching vocabulary will centre on concrete A1 nouns and short `olla` contexts. Every lesson will introduce at most ten typed lexical entries; other context will be reused through declared prerequisites or visibly supplied.
- The 96 Focused questions allow recognition, controlled choice, Finnish and English production, and misconception contrasts. The 24-question Review provides retrieval and transfer without introducing plural or case inflection.
- The pack assesses controlled written grammar, not listening, speaking, pronunciation, spontaneous interaction, or overall CEFR ability.

No unresolved high-impact scope, prerequisite, sequence, vocabulary-load, or count issue remains before authoring.

## Final gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The finished topology is five Focused lessons and tests followed by one Review. The Focused counts are 20, 24, 16, 20, and 16; the Review contains 24 questions and mixes only the five taught skills.
- Target coverage is 24 singular-form items, 30 reference-choice items, 20 independent-use items, 26 noun-modifier items, and 20 standard person-reference items.
- All 120 scored items use complete sentences. The response distribution is exactly 24 each of multiple choice, fill-in, Finnish construction, Finnish-to-English translation, and word order; the 20 optional items also use all five formats.
- The first lesson now visibly supplies the identity-sentence frame and asks only for form recall; reference selection, independent use, noun modification, and standard person reference remain owned by their later Focused lessons.
- The vocabulary ledger contains 20 introduced and 29 reused entries with no hidden supplied vocabulary. Every reused entry resolves through the prerequisite chain, and no lesson introduces more than ten words.
- Standard-written `hän` for a person and `se` for a non-human referent are both assessed. Colloquial `se` for people is explained as deferred and is never an accepted answer.
- Natural English now distinguishes independent `that`/`it`, “that … over there,” and a previously identified noun phrase without the former literal word salad. Finnish-to-English items accept natural alternatives where `se` permits them.
- All 28 multiple-choice items, including optional practice, give each wrong option distinct form-specific feedback. Every scored item retains its misconception category, complete sentence explanation, and mutual same-skill/same-format different-answer mastery partner; no same-skill Finnish sentence repeats within one response type.
- Direct-source validation and the demonstrative-family validator pass. Thirteen family unit cases—twelve negative mutations plus the complete-family acceptance case—cover topology, initial form support, Review ownership, scope, language quality, distinctness, feedback, register, question and inessive boundaries, and mastery pairs.
- The complete client gate passes 169 unit and 14 integration tests. Six dedicated browser cases pass across the configured 320-, 768-, and 1440-pixel projects, including all five demonstrative routes, exact topology, responsive catalog cards, and the longest learning map.

## Final decision

The count and split are approved for controlled `0 - A1.3` written grammar practice. General textual reference, colloquial person reference, non-nominative cases, listening, speaking, free interaction, calibrated difficulty, and overall CEFR ability remain outside scope.
