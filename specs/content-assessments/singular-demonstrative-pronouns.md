## Important-skill coverage and sequence

1. Recognise and produce `tämä`, `tuo`, and `se`.
2. Choose among a nearby referent, a farther jointly visible referent, and a referent identifiable to the listener or from context.
3. Use a singular demonstrative independently.
4. Use a singular demonstrative before a nominative singular noun.
5. Distinguish standard-written `se` for a non-human referent from `hän` for a person without claiming that `tämä` and `tuo` cannot identify people.

The final Review mixes only these five decisions. General textual-reference nuance, article-like `se`, colloquial person reference, and every non-nominative form are excluded.

## Pre-authoring gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The boundary is coherent and suitable for a first demonstrative pack. Form recognition precedes reference choice, then independent and noun-modifying use, then the standard-register `se`/`hän` contrast.
- Each Focused target asks for one new decision. Complete sentence frames, `olla` forms, and supporting noun forms will be visibly supplied whenever they are not the target.
- Likely errors are translating English “that” mechanically, confusing `tuo` with context-known `se`, inserting an unnecessary noun, omitting the noun after a modifier, and using colloquial `se` for a person in standard writing. Lessons and diagnostics will address each error directly.
- Teaching vocabulary will centre on concrete A1 nouns and short `olla` contexts. Every lesson will introduce at most ten typed lexical entries; other context will be reused through declared prerequisites or visibly supplied.
- The 96 Focused questions allow recognition, controlled choice, Finnish and English production, and misconception contrasts. The 24-question Review provides retrieval and transfer without introducing plural or case inflection.
- The pack assesses controlled written grammar, not listening, speaking, pronunciation, spontaneous interaction, or overall CEFR ability.

No unresolved high-impact scope, prerequisite, sequence, vocabulary-load, or count issue remains before authoring.
