## Important-skill coverage and sequence

1. Build singular negative demonstrative statements with `ei ole`.
2. Build plural negative demonstrative statements with `eivät ole`.
3. Transform a visibly supplied affirmative demonstrative statement into its negative counterpart while preserving number.

The Review mixes these three negative skills only. Demonstrative reference choice is visibly supplied when negation is the target.

## Pre-authoring gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The boundary mirrors the existing negative-`olla` learner operation while limiting subjects to standard demonstratives.
- Singular precedes plural; transformation follows after both negative frames are taught. Each Focused item assesses polarity or number agreement, not an additional hidden reference decision.
- Likely errors are using `eivät` with a singular demonstrative, using `ei` with a plural demonstrative, keeping affirmative `on` or `ovat`, omitting `ole`, and changing the demonstrative during transformation.
- Demonstrative forms and contextual nouns will be translated or supplied where they are not assessed. Typed vocabulary remains at or below ten new entries per lesson.
- Sixty-four Focused questions cover form choice and controlled production; the 24-question Review supplies mixed retrieval and transfer without padding the narrow boundary.
- The pack excludes questions, other verbs and tenses, colloquial Finnish, and communicative or pronunciation assessment.

No unresolved high-impact issue remains before authoring.

## Final gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The finished topology is three Focused lessons and tests followed by one Review. The Focused counts are 20, 24, and 20; the Review contains 24 questions and mixes only the three taught skills.
- Target coverage is 28 singular `ei ole` items, 32 plural `eivät ole` items, and 28 controlled affirmative-to-negative transformations. The transformation set includes both number patterns.
- All 88 scored items use complete sentences. The response distribution is 16 multiple choice, 18 fill-in, 20 Finnish construction, 18 Finnish-to-English translation, and 16 word order; the 12 optional items use all five formats.
- The vocabulary ledger contains 16 introduced and 20 reused entries with no hidden supplied vocabulary. Every reused entry resolves through the prerequisite chain, and no lesson introduces more than ten words.
- The singular Focused test requires `ei ole`, the plural Focused test requires `eivät ole`, and accepted answers never retain `on` or `ovat` as the negative predicate. Questions remain outside this pack.
- Literal demonstrative glosses and implausible “house at home” combinations are removed. Natural English keeps reference information in the cue or uses a previously identified noun phrase without distorting Finnish negation.
- All 19 multiple-choice items, including optional practice, give each wrong option distinct feedback about demonstrative choice, singular/plural negative agreement, retained affirmative forms, or missing `ole`. Every scored item retains its misconception category, complete sentence explanation, and mutual same-skill/same-format different-answer mastery partner; no same-skill Finnish sentence repeats within one response type.
- Direct-source validation and the demonstrative-family validator pass. Thirteen family unit cases—twelve negative mutations plus the complete-family acceptance case—cover topology, initial form support, Review ownership, scope, language quality, distinctness, feedback, register, question and inessive boundaries, and mastery pairs.
- The complete client gate passes 169 unit and 14 integration tests. Six dedicated browser cases pass across the configured 320-, 768-, and 1440-pixel projects, including all five demonstrative routes, exact topology, responsive catalog cards, and the longest learning map.

## Final decision

The 88-question count is approved because it covers three narrow negative-statement decisions with a separate mixed retrieval pass without padding. Other verbs, other tenses, questions, spoken Finnish, listening, speaking, free interaction, calibrated difficulty, and overall CEFR ability remain outside scope.
