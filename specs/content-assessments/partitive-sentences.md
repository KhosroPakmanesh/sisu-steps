# Partitive in everyday sentences — pedagogy assessment

## Topic, level and scope

Pack `partitive-sentences`, version 1.0.0; beginner written grammar within `0 - A1.3`. The approved topic contains singular and plural material with independent Focused preparation. All Finnish is standard written Finnish; explanatory prose is English.

## Sources and coverage blueprint

The [shared partitive assessment](partitive-family.md#verified-sources-and-decisions) records verified sources and their scope. The [complete target inventory](partitive-family.md#coverage-blueprint-and-proposed-counts) records every lesson and proposed count before bulk authoring.

Paired singular/plural substance or indefinite objects, familiar verb objects, negative objects and location existence/absence surround separate language-name and ongoing-activity targets. Language names and the bounded ongoing activity are singular-only targets.

Supplied verb and location forms keep the scored decision on the nominal case/number. Full explanations distinguish action objects from the introduced or absent item in an existence construction.

Proposed and authored inventory: 10 lessons and Focused tests, 232 Focused questions, one 40-question cumulative Review, 272 scored questions overall and 30 optional questions. Each lesson has three optional items and at most ten new lexical entries. Prerequisite chains resolve inside this pack; the first target introduces its pattern independently.
