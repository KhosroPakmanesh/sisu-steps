## Important-skill coverage and sequence

The seven Focused targets are singular personal pronouns, plural personal pronouns, pronoun reference and polite `te`, singular affirmative `olla`, plural affirmative `olla`, affirmative pronoun–verb agreement, and subject-pronoun use. Two Reviews follow all seven targets and mix recognition, agreement, complete statements, gender-neutral `hän` and `he`, plural `te`, and polite-singular `te` without introducing negation or questions.

## Pre-authoring gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The boundary represents one coherent beginner achievement: identify the six personal pronouns and use them with affirmative present-tense `olla` statements.
- The sequence moves from pronoun meaning and reference through singular and plural forms to mixed agreement and controlled subject-pronoun use.
- The existing seven target-specific lessons and 168 Focused exercises already teach one decision at a time. Their 40-question agreement Review plus twenty affirmative-target items from the former polarity Review and fourteen same-target items from the former whole-topic final Review provide two distinct retrieval passes containing 74 purposeful Review questions.
- Complete English meanings remain visible before Finnish sentence construction. Pronoun-only recognition and isolated-form items remain intentional exceptions because morphology or reference is the stated target.
- Vocabulary remains controlled through the existing invariant locations, states, occupations, and names. Contextual meanings are supplied when vocabulary is not assessed.
- Likely errors include confusing `hän` with a gendered English pronoun, treating one `olla` form as universal, mismatching plural pronouns and verbs, and giving polite-singular `te` singular agreement. Existing lessons, diagnostics, and Review retrieval address them.
- The 242-question count follows the seven-skill coverage already authored; it is not being rounded to a common pack size. The repository-wide fixed 200-question floor will be replaced by an evidence-based non-empty upper-bounded rule so narrower coherent packs are not padded.
- The pack is standard Finnish only. It excludes spoken forms, listening, pronunciation, free conversation, other verbs, other tenses, and personal-pronoun cases.

No unresolved high-impact scope, prerequisite, sequencing, or count issue remains. The split may proceed while preserving every scored and optional exercise and keeping all stable IDs globally unique.
