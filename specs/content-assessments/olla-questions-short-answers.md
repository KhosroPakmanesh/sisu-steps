- Affirmative questions precede negative questions, singular work precedes plural work within each polarity, and short answers follow the complete question sequence.
- Required statement forms are shown and explained inside the applicable question lessons instead of being treated as unstated cross-pack knowledge. The first question lesson therefore has no external declared prerequisite; later lessons depend only on skills taught earlier in this pack.
- The existing 120 Focused exercises, 40-question question-and-answer Review, and eighteen question-target transfer items from the former whole-topic final Review provide 178 purposeful scored questions across two retrieval passes. The total reflects five distinct targets and does not require filler.
- Complete English meanings remain visible before Finnish sentence construction. Question-particle attachment, word order, person agreement, negative question forms, and response polarity are made explicit.
- Likely errors include attaching `-ko/-kö` to the pronoun, using statement word order, losing person agreement, omitting `ole` in negative questions, and answering a negative question with the wrong polarity. Existing lessons, distractors, diagnostics, and Reviews address them.
- Vocabulary remains controlled and unchanged. The pack is standard Finnish only and excludes spoken question contractions, informal short answers, content questions, other verbs, other tenses, listening, pronunciation, and free interaction.

No unresolved high-impact scope, prerequisite, sequencing, or count issue remains. The split may proceed while preserving every scored and optional exercise and keeping all stable IDs globally unique.
