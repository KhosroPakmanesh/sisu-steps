# Partitive with numbers and quantities — pedagogy assessment

## Topic, level and scope

Pack `partitive-quantities`, version 1.0.0; beginner written grammar within `0 - A1.3`. The approved topic contains singular and plural material with independent Focused preparation. All Finnish is standard written Finnish; explanatory prose is English.

## Sources and coverage blueprint

The [shared partitive assessment](partitive-family.md#verified-sources-and-decisions) records verified sources and their scope. The [complete target inventory](partitive-family.md#coverage-blueprint-and-proposed-counts) records every lesson and proposed count before bulk authoring.

Counting expressions, measurements, uncounted singular amounts and countable plural amounts are separate targets. Singular/plural amounts are paired in this pack. Counting means grammatical singular after basic numbers greater than one and monta, regardless of referent count.

Complete quantity phrases are intentional. Finnish noun alternatives and the quantity frame are supplied for case selection; English-meaning tasks recall the locally classified quantity and noun words.

Proposed and authored inventory: 4 lessons and Focused tests, 88 Focused questions, one 16-question cumulative Review, 104 scored questions overall and 12 optional questions. Each lesson has three optional items and at most ten new lexical entries. Prerequisite chains resolve inside this pack; the first target introduces its pattern independently.
