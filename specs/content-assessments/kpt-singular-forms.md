# KPT and singular forms — pedagogy assessment

## Identity and proposed scope

- **Pack ID:** `kpt-singular-forms`
- **Topic:** strong and weak KPT grades, the beginner KPT families, genitive noun forms, and present-tense `minä` forms
- **Level:** `0 - A1.3`
- **Proposed size:** 260 scored exercises, eight Focused tests, three Reviews, eight lessons, and 32 optional practice exercises
- **Register:** standard written Finnish only

## Important-skill sequence

1. `Strong and weak KPT grades` — understand the two related consonant grades without treating either as more correct.
2. `KPT double consonants` — `kk → k`, `pp → p`, and `tt → t`.
3. `KPT common single consonants` — the declared beginner single-consonant changes.
4. `KPT special k changes` — the two explicitly taught lexical families.
5. `KPT consonant clusters` — the declared cluster changes.
6. `KPT recognition` — distinguish the learned families in fixed authored examples.
7. `Genitive -n` — build controlled singular noun forms while separating the supplied ending from KPT.
8. `Minä verb forms with KPT` — build and use controlled present-tense forms in complete sentences.

The three Reviews separately consolidate KPT families, singular form building, and final transfer.

## Pre-authoring assessment — 2026-09-05

**Disposition: approved with limitations.**

This is intentionally the largest successor pack because it contains the greatest number of visible patterns and two applications. The boundary remains coherent: every exercise asks how a known word moves between a strong and weak grade in a controlled singular form. Eight single-target Focused tests prevent noun and verb endings from becoming hidden secondary decisions.

Resolved findings:

- Strong/weak grade receives its own first-principles lesson and Focused test instead of being assumed inside the double-consonant lesson.
- KPT-only noun production visibly supplies genitive `-n`; genitive-focused production supplies the necessary stem. KPT-only verb production visibly supplies the stem frame, `minä`, and `-n` ending.
- Verb usage remains in short complete sentences with fixed translated time, place, or manner context. Object government, other verb types, and spoken forms are excluded.
- Each difficult KPT lesson retains four or five optional practice exercises, at least two contrasts, and no more than ten introduced words.
- The 260-question proposal supports contrast within each family, recognition, controlled production, misconception diagnosis, and three distinct retrieval boundaries without padding.

Migration resolution:

- Existing KPT, genitive, and verb lesson, test, practice, and scored-exercise IDs move unchanged to this pack.
- Existing `foundations-review` exercises with KPT, genitive, or `minä`-verb targets move once into the new Reviews.
- The former broad topic note moves unchanged to this largest successor pack. Lesson notes follow their lessons. Ordinary-test and split-review learner records are remapped rather than cleared.

Limitations: KPT is taught through controlled known families and is not presented as perfectly predictable for unfamiliar vocabulary. The pack excludes object cases, other persons and tenses, other verb types, spoken Finnish, listening, pronunciation, free composition, interaction, and general CEFR certification. Independent Finnish-teacher review remains recommended.

## Final assessment

**Disposition: approved with limitations.**

- Direct-source validation found no duplicate scored task, repeated same-type sentence task, unsupported KPT family, malformed mastery pair, hidden target meaning, or spoken-form answer. Existing KPT, genitive, and verb IDs are retained once within the successor family.

No unresolved high-impact content issue remains. The limitation is deliberate: the pack practises known standard-written-Finnish KPT families and does not claim full predictability for unfamiliar vocabulary, other persons or tenses, free composition, empirical calibration, CEFR certification, or independent professional Finnish-teacher review.
