# Finnish-teaching pedagogy assessment: inessive-demonstrative-forms

## Pack identity and counts

- Pack ID: `inessive-demonstrative-forms`
- Topic: singular and plural inessive demonstratives, independently and before matching nouns
- Level: `0 - A1.3`
- Proposed count: 120 scored exercises in four Focused tests of 24, 24, 20, and 24 questions followed by one 28-question Review; 16 optional practice exercises
- Final count: 120 scored exercises and 16 optional practice exercises

## Important-skill coverage and sequence

1. Recognise and produce singular `tässä`, `tuossa`, and `siinä`.
2. Recognise and produce plural `näissä`, `noissa`, and `niissä`.
3. Match a singular inessive demonstrative with a visibly supplied singular inessive noun.
4. Match a plural inessive demonstrative with a visibly supplied plural inessive noun.

The Review mixes these four skills. The title intentionally says inessive: every other demonstrative case and related adverb is excluded.

## Pre-authoring gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The boundary is coherent because it owns one case meaning, singular/plural demonstrative forms, and case-number agreement with supplied noun forms.
- Independent singular and plural forms precede noun-phrase agreement. Focused items never require the learner to derive a non-target noun stem or plural case ending.
- Likely errors are substituting the related adverbs `täällä`, `tuolla`, or `siellä`, keeping nominative forms, confusing `tässä` with `näissä`, and failing to match the demonstrative's number to the supplied noun.
- Concrete containers, rooms, texts, and objects will provide natural inside-location contexts. Non-target noun forms and meanings remain visibly supplied, with no more than ten new typed vocabulary entries per lesson.
- Ninety-two Focused questions cover irregular-looking form recognition and agreement; the 28-question Review provides retrieval and transfer without introducing elative, illative, or external-location cases.
- The pack assesses controlled written grammar only and excludes broader location adverbs, speech, listening, pronunciation, and spontaneous interaction.

No unresolved high-impact issue remains before authoring.

## Final gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The finished topology is four Focused lessons and tests followed by one Review. The Focused counts are 24, 24, 20, and 24; the Review contains 28 questions and mixes only the four taught skills.
- Target coverage is 32 singular independent inessive items, 32 plural independent inessive items, 26 singular modifier-agreement items, and 30 plural modifier-agreement items.
- All 120 scored items use complete sentences. The response distribution is 22 multiple choice, 24 fill-in, 24 Finnish construction, 26 Finnish-to-English translation, and 24 word order; the 16 optional items use all five formats.
- The vocabulary ledger contains 14 introduced and 26 reused entries with no hidden supplied vocabulary. Complete singular and plural inessive noun forms remain visible where noun inflection is not the target, and no lesson introduces more than ten words.
- Every Focused test covers all three forms in its number set: `tässä`, `tuossa`, `siinä` or `näissä`, `noissa`, `niissä`. Accepted answers never substitute broader `täällä`, `tuolla`, or `siellä` adverbs or introduce another demonstrative case.
- All 72 independent tasks—64 scored and eight optional practice—now identify a plausible bounded compartment, garage, district, or room before the response and match the cue to nearby, farther jointly visible, or previously identified reference. The noun-modifier tasks continue to name their containers directly.
- Finnish-to-English independent tasks keep precise `in this/that/it` or plural meanings and also accept natural `here`, `there`, or `over there` alternatives. Supplied plural forms now carry plural English glosses.
- All 25 multiple-choice items, including optional practice, give each wrong option distinct feedback about reference, nominative versus inessive case, or singular/plural agreement. Every scored item retains its misconception category, complete sentence explanation, and mutual same-skill/same-format different-answer mastery partner; no same-skill Finnish sentence repeats within one response type.
- Direct-source validation and the demonstrative-family validator pass. Thirteen family unit cases—twelve negative mutations plus the complete-family acceptance case—cover topology, initial form support, Review ownership, scope, language quality, distinctness, feedback, register, question and inessive boundaries, and mastery pairs.
- The complete client gate passes 169 unit and 14 integration tests. Six dedicated browser cases pass across the configured 320-, 768-, and 1440-pixel projects, including all five demonstrative routes, exact topology, responsive catalog cards, and the longest learning map.

## Final decision

The count and split are approved for controlled `0 - A1.3` written grammar practice. Other demonstrative cases, unsupplied noun inflection, broader location adverbs, listening, speaking, free interaction, calibrated difficulty, and overall CEFR ability remain outside scope.
