# Genitive family — pedagogy assessment

## Scope and authorization

Date: 2026-10-20. The user approved the revised eight-pack, 42-lesson outline and
asked for implementation using the repository content skill and Partitive style.
REQ-G001-154 owns this addition. Existing content, versions, learner data and
application behavior remain unchanged.

This is a written-grammar curriculum beginning at zero and continuing beyond
A1.3. Singular formation packs use `0 - A1.3`; systematic plural formation packs
use `A1.3 - A2.2`; mixed usage packs use `0 - A2.2`. These are instructional
estimates, not certified CEFR outcomes. Within mixed packs, singular foundations
precede their plural partners. The explicitly approved formation-first catalog
order has five formation packs followed by three usage packs.

## Prerequisites, vocabulary and misconceptions

Each pack is independently teachable: local prerequisites refer only to earlier
lessons in that pack. General KPT and personal possessive teaching remains in its
existing packs. New formation lessons explicitly explain every assessed stem
change. Usage lessons provide complete supporting noun forms, verb forms and
meanings rather than relying on undeclared outside-pack recall.

Morphology inventories use at most ten new lexical words per lesson. Base words
are vocabulary, not grammar-only exemptions; supplied dictionary forms and meanings
make case derivation a grammatical demand with no lexical-recall entry. Usage
lessons classify each encountered lexical word, keep new recall to ten words, and
show supporting forms and meanings. Translation into English inventories every
supporting word actually recalled. Transparent phrases are separate lexical words;
no convenient multiword bundle is treated as a fixed expression.

The question inventory is checked independently from declarations using the actual
prompt, options, tokens, answers and aligned sentence parts. Non-sentence forms and
inflection identity require manual review in addition to automated coverage.

Principal misconceptions: attaching n to every dictionary form; assuming all i or
s words share a stem; copying the singular weak grade into plural jen/ien forms;
rejecting standard variants; confusing form number with owner or owned-object
number; putting a genitive after its head noun; omitting adjective agreement;
using nominative demonstratives before genitive nouns; inventing a plural form
of minkä; treating täytyy as a person-conjugated verb; and interpreting every
n-ending as ownership or every affirmative object as a total object.

Meaning, formation and exceptions belong in grammar sections. Dedicated vocabulary
areas own lexical lists. Worked examples demonstrate the current target with
complete meanings and construction steps. Summaries state one bounded rule and
one example, following the Partitive presentation style. Optional practice applies
already taught material. Reviews introduce no grammar or vocabulary.

Formation tasks use recognition, typed production, diagnostic repair and structural
comparisons; fewer formats are intentional for isolated morphology. Usage targets
use sentence completion, choices, construction, translation and error analysis
where suitable, with number-specific preparation and visible reference cues.
Singular/plural refers to the assessed nominal form except necessity, where the
person scope is explicit. A plural owner can own one item.

Personal-pronoun possessive suffixes, personal pronouns with kanssa, comprehensive
object-case selection, passive/imperative objects, archaic forms and exhaustive
word-family paradigms are excluded. Necessity supplies the personal genitives and
infinitives. Object recognition is restricted to explicitly bounded, affirmative
active sentences referring to one whole countable object.

### Language, teaching and cognitive-load review

The finite [target inventory](../../client/src/features/learning/shared/content/validation/genitive-family-targets.ts)
records every assessed dictionary-form/inflected-form or phrase mapping. Formation
examples explain each stem change, including reverse strengthening in osoite,
tuote, soitin and keitin. The two meanings of kuusi are distinguished. Plural
alternatives include -iden/-itten, the taught longer-word -jen alternatives,
-sten/-sien, -inten/-imien and selected consonant/vowel stems. Poetic and archaic
forms are outside the declared register. The [Jyväskylä kuka paradigm](https://users.jyu.fi/~pamakine/kieli/suomi/pronominit/pronkukaen.html)
also supports accepting keitten alongside the keiden model.

The 673 morphology items deliberately assess isolated formation, reverse
recognition, repair or a two-word comparison. Their supplied Finnish form and
English meaning make lexical-recall arrays empty. The 557 sentence/phrase items
have complete aligned construction explanations. Supporting inflected noun and
verb forms are visible before Finnish completion; the 139 Finnish-to-English
items instead inventory all supporting base words that must be interpreted.
Runtime and direct-source checks compare those recall lists with the actual
aligned sentence and linked vocabulary. After correction, neither missing nor
unnecessary recall entries remain in this bounded inventory.

Grammar sections own meanings, rules and paradigms; the vocabulary area owns
standalone lexical lists. Contextual translations and inflection tables are
intentional grammar evidence. Each preparation's summary states its bounded rule
with a translated example. Singular/plural metadata identifies the assessed
genitive form; necessity explicitly identifies the corresponding person number.
Plural modifiers sometimes accompany a singular head noun to demonstrate that
owner/related-noun number and owned-object number are independent.

The five morphology packs use two interaction types as a deliberate exception:
sentence ordering would assess another skill. Their tasks still distinguish
recognition, production, repair, reverse recognition and paired retrieval. The
usage packs use choices, completion, Finnish production and English interpretation.
Scored totals are 339 choices, 607 completions, 19 Finnish productions and 139
English interpretations. Optional practice has 126 completions; this includes
ending/partial-form guidance, paired retrieval and sentence repair. Reviews use
taught vocabulary and constructions. The four special-stem Review comparisons
use choices so they do not merely rename the same Focused typed comparisons.
Focused mastery partners are reciprocal, assess the same target with different
answers, and are separated by at least four positions.
