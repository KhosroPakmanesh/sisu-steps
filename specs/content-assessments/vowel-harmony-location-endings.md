# Vowel harmony and location endings — pedagogy assessment

## Identity and proposed scope

- **Pack ID:** `vowel-harmony-location-endings`
- **Topic:** vowel families, neutral-vowel behavior, and written inessive `-ssa/-ssä` forms
- **Level:** `0 - A1.3`
- **Proposed size:** 110 scored exercises, four Focused tests, two Reviews, four lessons, and 14 optional practice exercises
- **Register:** standard written Finnish only

## Authoritative sources

- [Uusi kielemme: Vowel Harmony](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-grammar/vowel-harmony-vokaaliharmonia-finnish-grammar)
- [Uusi kielemme: Beginner Finnish Topics A1](https://uusikielemme.fi/language-levels/beginner-finnish-topics-level-a1-a1-1-to-a1-3)

## Important-skill sequence

1. `Vowel harmony` — distinguish back- and front-vowel words.
2. `Neutral-vowel harmony` — handle `e` and `i` alone and alongside back or front vowels.
3. `Inessive -ssa/-ssä` — select and attach the correct written location ending to stable stems.
4. `Inessive location sentences` — use the learned form in short supplied-context sentences.

The first Review consolidates classification and ending choice. The second mixes controlled forms and short location statements without adding another case ending.

## Pre-authoring assessment — 2026-09-05

**Disposition: approved with limitations.**

The boundary is coherent because every target answers the same beginner question: which harmonic ending belongs on this word? Separating neutral-vowel behavior resolves an underdeveloped point in the former ten-question vowel test. Moving from word classification to stable-stem `-ssa/-ssä` forms and then complete location statements supplies a clear recognition-to-production progression.

Resolved findings:

- Focused inessive work will use stable stems only. KPT, `-e` stem changes, compound-word harmony, loanword exceptions, elative forms, and other location cases remain outside the assessed boundary.
- Sentence prompts will display their complete intended English meaning before response. `on` and non-target context words will be visibly supplied rather than treated as hidden grammar.
- Each Focused lesson introduces no more than ten scored words; familiar words recur across formats before new context is added.
- The 110-question proposal reflects four distinct decisions plus two retrieval stages. It is not a quota, and duplicate normalized tasks or surface-only paraphrases are rejected.

Migration resolution:

- Existing vowel-harmony and inessive lesson, test, practice, and scored-exercise IDs move unchanged to this pack.
- Existing `foundations-review` exercises with vowel-harmony or inessive targets move once into the new Reviews.
- Existing ordinary-test and split-review learner records are remapped to their new topic and test owners rather than cleared.

Limitations: the pack teaches controlled written form recognition and production. It does not assess spoken Finnish, listening, pronunciation, compound-word exceptions, free composition, interaction, or general CEFR ability. Independent Finnish-teacher review remains recommended.
