## Sources

- [Kielitoimiston ohjepankki: possessive suffixes](https://kielitoimistonohjepankki.fi/ohje/omistusliitteet/): endings identify the owner; first-/second-person pronouns are usually optional; third-person reference depends on construction.
- [University of Turku: Mun talo vai minun taloni?](https://blogit.utu.fi/suomeaedistyneille/2017/06/06/mun-talo-vai-minun-taloni/): nominative plural `-t` disappears before a possessive suffix; the verb/context distinguishes singular from plural possessed objects. The article contains a mismatched owner in one unrelated example; that example is not reused.
- [Finnish National Agency for Education: pronoun exercises](https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/136294_lisatehtavia_meidan_koulu.pdf): plural `kenen` questions use an unsuffixed plural noun, while ownership answers use a possessive suffix.
- [Kielitoimiston: owner pronoun and suffix reference](https://kielitoimistonohjepankki.fi/ohje/omistusliitteet-persoonapronomini-ja-omistusliite-laulaja-ja-hanen-puolisonsa/): retain a third-person owner in the bounded identification pattern; avoid a universal ban on third-person omission.

Disposition: **approved with limitations**.

The important new skills are **Plural possessed nouns in ownership sentences** and **Plural whose questions with kenen**. The first Focused unit has 24 questions: eight recognition tasks, eight noun completions, and eight complete-sentence constructions. Six owner forms and four personal plus both third-person harmony endings receive recognition and production; supporting owner/suffix/frame choices are visible so dropping `-t` is the assessed change. The second has 12 questions: four recognition, four construction, and four word-order tasks, with supplied complete plural noun forms and agreement. Its single target is the bounded plural owner-question pattern, including the unsuffixed noun. Question-to-answer combination waits for Review.

The 24-question Review retrieves eight first-/second-person omissions, four third-person reference decisions, four singular/plural English interpretations, four plural questions and four ownership replies. Those subpoints reuse prior rules, not new grammatical topics. Three lessons each have three optional items, for **60 scored and nine optional** questions. Pair each task with the same response demand and target but a different model answer. This quantity covers contrasts and controlled recognition/production rather than expanding to the 1,000-question ceiling.

Recommend the existing possessive and plural-demonstrative packs. The current dependency model is pack-local: do not invent external prerequisite metadata or silently assume vocabulary provenance. Re-establish six familiar lexical bases (`pallo, kirja, auto, kynä, tyyny, peli`) in the first lesson, reuse them through the local declared skill chain, and visibly supply their non-target plural forms. All six bases fit below the ten-word limit. Grammar-only pronouns, demonstratives, interrogatives and verb forms stay in grammar teaching. Owner/suffix mapping and omission/reference are explicit supporting reminders in the first lesson, preceding mixed Review retrieval; Review prompts supply non-target grammar where needed. Focused tests reference only their own lesson.

Grammar sections own formation and supporting rules, vocabulary cards own lexical lists, examples demonstrate all six owners and relevant number/harmony contrasts, common mistakes diagnose `pallotsi`, missing suffixes, wrong `on/ovat`, and third-person ambiguity. Optional practice differs from scored prompts. Construction prompts show complete English meanings; second-person prompts identify one person or a group. English third-person translations explicitly accept his/her/his or her. No new vocabulary or grammar first appears in Review.

The initial blueprint placed supplied plural examples in the existing omission lesson. The user's later scope correction below supersedes this placement: all plural omission teaching belongs in the new pack.

Limitations: regular nominative noun forms, standard written Finnish and controlled beginner production. Exclude irregular stems, other plural cases, negative possession, spoken forms, polite singular address, complex reflexive/reference structures, listening/speaking, empirical difficulty calibration and independent professional teacher review. No unresolved high-impact finding remains at the pre-authoring gate. Final approval requires source and rendered all-item inspection plus technical checks.
