# How and why questions

Current status, 2026-10-29: the independent audit required corrections. The

`how-and-why-questions`, version `1.0.0`, level `0 - A1.3`.

Three lessons cover manner, degree and because-replies. See the shared
[blueprint, sources, both pedagogy decisions and final evidence](question-words-family.md).
This pack owns 72 scored questions and nine independent optional items. Numerical
measurements are bounded; the pack does not teach all number formation. Existing
packs and compatible progress remain unchanged; no migration or reset is introduced.
