## Pre-authoring gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The boundary represents one coherent beginner achievement: construct and interpret negative present-tense `olla` statements.
- Singular negation precedes plural negation; controlled affirmative-to-negative transformation follows only after both negative paradigms have been taught.
- Personal pronouns and affirmative source forms are supporting information rather than hidden assessed prerequisites. Pronoun meanings and any affirmative source sentence remain visible where needed, while the pack teaches negative agreement from first principles.
- The existing 72 Focused exercises, twenty negative-target questions from the former polarity Review, and eight negative-target transfer items from the former whole-topic final Review provide 100 purposeful scored questions. Adding questions merely to reach the former 200-question floor would repeat narrow agreement decisions without adding evidence.
- Complete English meanings remain visible before Finnish construction or completion. Isolated `en ole` through `eivät ole` mappings are retained only where forming the negative morphology is explicitly the target.
- Likely errors include using `ei` for every person, omitting `ole`, retaining an affirmative `olla` form after the negative verb, and mismatching singular and plural subjects. Existing lessons, distractors, diagnostics, and mixed Review practice address them.
- Vocabulary remains controlled and unchanged. The pack is standard Finnish only and excludes spoken forms, questions, short answers, other verbs, other tenses, listening, pronunciation, and free interaction.

No unresolved high-impact scope, prerequisite, sequencing, or count issue remains. The split may proceed while preserving every scored and optional exercise and keeping all stable IDs globally unique.
