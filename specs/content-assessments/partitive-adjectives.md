Version 1.1.0 adds the independently prepared plural predicate lesson after the singular substance predicate lesson. The [expansion assessment](partitive-expansion.md) records the vocabulary, sentence-feedback, alternative, preservation and validation evidence. Original teaching/question objects remain intact. The changed pack version intentionally clears old scored progress and completions while retaining notes.

The sections below retain the original 1.0.0 assessment as dated evidence.

## Topic, level and scope

Pack `partitive-adjectives`, version 1.0.0; beginner written grammar within `0 - A1.3`. The approved topic contains singular and plural material with independent Focused preparation. All Finnish is standard written Finnish; explanatory prose is English.

## Sources and coverage blueprint

The [shared partitive assessment](partitive-family.md#verified-sources-and-decisions) records verified sources and their scope. The [complete target inventory](partitive-family.md#coverage-blueprint-and-proposed-counts) records every lesson and proposed count before bulk authoring.

Singular attributive agreement precedes plural attributive agreement; describing a substance with a singular predicative adjective has separate preparation. Case/number agreement is distinguished from visually identical suffixes.

The noun and adjective alternatives are supplied for the agreement choice. Substance subjects remain nominative; the adjective describes the substance after on or ei ole.

Proposed and authored inventory: 3 lessons and Focused tests, 68 Focused questions, one 12-question cumulative Review, 80 scored questions overall and 9 optional questions. Each lesson has three optional items and at most ten new lexical entries. Prerequisite chains resolve inside this pack; the first target introduces its pattern independently.
