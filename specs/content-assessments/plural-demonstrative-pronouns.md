## Important-skill coverage and sequence

1. Recognise and produce `nämä`, `nuo`, and `ne`.
2. Choose the plural form for nearby, farther jointly visible, or identifiable referents.
3. Use a plural demonstrative independently.
4. Use a plural demonstrative before a nominative plural noun.
5. Match a plural demonstrative with a visibly supplied T-plural noun rather than a singular noun.
6. Distinguish standard-written `ne` for non-human referents from `he` for people.

The Review mixes only these skills. The pack does not teach how to form arbitrary noun plurals, non-nominative plural cases, or colloquial person reference.

## Pre-authoring gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The plural boundary is coherent because it owns plural reference and visible demonstrative–noun number agreement while supplying non-target noun stems and endings.
- Form recognition and reference choice precede independent use, noun modification, agreement, and the `ne`/`he` register contrast.
- Likely errors are using a singular demonstrative for several referents, choosing `nuo` solely from English “those,” pairing `nämä` with a singular noun, overlooking the noun's `-t`, and using colloquial `ne` for people in standard writing.
- Concrete plural nouns and familiar `olla` frames will keep vocabulary within ten new typed entries per lesson. Noun forms will be supplied when plural formation is not the target.
- The 120 Focused questions give the three-way choice and agreement targets more evidence than simple independent use. The 24-question Review supports mixed retrieval without introducing a new case or noun-formation requirement.
- Limitations are controlled written practice, authored difficulty, and no listening, speaking, pronunciation, free interaction, or overall CEFR claim.

No unresolved high-impact issue remains before authoring.

## Final gate

**Disposition: approved with limitations**

- The finished topology is six Focused lessons and tests followed by one Review. The Focused counts are 20, 24, 16, 20, 24, and 16; the Review contains 24 questions and mixes only the six taught skills.
- Target coverage is 24 plural-form items, 28 reference-choice items, 20 independent-use items, 24 noun-modifier items, 28 number-agreement items, and 20 standard person-reference items.
- All 144 scored items use complete sentences. The response distribution is 30 multiple choice, 30 fill-in, 30 Finnish construction, 26 Finnish-to-English translation, and 28 word order; the 24 optional items use all five formats.
- The first lesson now visibly supplies the plural identity-sentence frame and asks only for form recall; later Focused lessons own reference, syntax, number agreement, and person-reference decisions.
- The vocabulary ledger contains 17 introduced and 43 reused entries with no hidden supplied vocabulary. Every reused entry resolves through the prerequisite chain, and no lesson introduces more than ten words.
- Standard-written `he` for people and `ne` for non-human referents are both assessed. Colloquial `ne` for people is explained as deferred and is never an accepted answer.
- Natural English uses “those books over there,” previously identified noun phrases, and independent `those`/`they` alternatives; plural supplied-form glosses now say “books,” “cars,” “keys,” and so on rather than singular meanings.
- All 34 multiple-choice items, including optional practice, give each wrong option distinct form- or agreement-specific feedback. Every scored item retains its misconception category, complete sentence explanation, and mutual same-skill/same-format different-answer mastery partner; no same-skill Finnish sentence repeats within one response type.
- Direct-source validation and the demonstrative-family validator pass. Thirteen family unit cases—twelve negative mutations plus the complete-family acceptance case—cover topology, initial form support, Review ownership, scope, language quality, distinctness, feedback, register, question and inessive boundaries, and mastery pairs.
