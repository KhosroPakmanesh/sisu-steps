# T-plural and plural agreement — pedagogy assessment

## Identity and proposed scope

- **Pack ID:** `t-plural-agreement`
- **Topic:** nominative T-plural recognition and formation, KPT in T-plurals, third-person plural endings, and short plural-subject sentences
- **Level:** `0 - A1.3`
- **Proposed size:** 200 scored exercises, six Focused tests, two Reviews, six lessons, and 20 optional practice exercises
- **Register:** standard written Finnish only

## Important-skill sequence

1. `T-plural recognition` — distinguish a supplied singular noun from its plural counterpart.
2. `Regular T-plural` — add plural `-t` to stable beginner stems.
3. `KPT T-plural recognition` — notice the strong-to-weak change in known plural pairs.
4. `T-plural with KPT` — produce controlled weak-grade T-plurals.
5. `Third-person plural -vat/-vät` — select the written plural verb ending from the supplied stem.
6. `Plural subject + ovat` — build short plural-subject sentences with the supplied verb and context.

The first Review consolidates noun forms. The second mixes noun plurality and controlled agreement without introducing plural cases or object rules.

## Pre-authoring assessment — 2026-09-05

**Disposition: approved with limitations.**

The boundary is coherent because all six targets express “more than one” in beginner written sentences. Separate recognition tests reduce the burden before production, and noun plurality precedes verb agreement. KPT is retaught only in the narrow form needed for T-plural nouns so the pack remains independently usable without an unsupported cross-pack prerequisite.

Resolved findings:

- Scope is limited to nominative T-plural subjects. Plural partitive, plural location cases, object plurality, plurale-tantum vocabulary, and advanced stem types are excluded.
- KPT production visibly supplies plural `-t`; ending-focused work supplies any non-target stem. `-vat/-vät` exercises supply the complete verb stem.
- Sentence tasks display the complete English meaning before Finnish construction and use stable noun stems or visibly supplied plural forms when agreement is the target.
- The 200-question proposal supports six distinct decisions, noun-form retrieval, and sentence transfer without forcing equal counts with the other packs.

Migration resolution:

- Existing T-plural, third-person plural, and plural-sentence lesson, test, practice, and scored-exercise IDs move unchanged to this pack.
- Existing `foundations-review` exercises with plural targets move once into the new Reviews.
- Existing ordinary-test and split-review learner records are remapped to their new owners. Lesson notes follow their lessons.

Limitations: the pack is controlled standard-written-Finnish practice, not an exhaustive account of Finnish plurality. It does not assess spoken Finnish, listening, pronunciation, free composition, interaction, advanced plural forms, or general CEFR ability. Independent Finnish-teacher review remains recommended.
