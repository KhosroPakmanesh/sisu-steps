# G003 requirements

G003 establishes catalog/topic separation. Later [REQ-G005-012](../G005-interactive-nordic-workbook-world/requirements.md#page-scene-requirements) refines REQ-G003-001: compact home cards omit objectives and detailed statistics, which remain on the topic route. [G007](../G007-scalable-stats-navigation/requirements.md) supersedes the former Reports/Data presentation preserved by REQ-G003-008. REQ-G003-011–015 refine catalog availability through disclosures, index-first metadata loading, viewport alignment, and matching Stats topic groups; card requirements apply when their owning group is open. REQ-G003-016/017 permit explicitly opened group and pack grammar references while preserving the catalog’s separation from full lessons and tests. Other navigation and learner-data boundaries remain in force.

## Functional requirements

- **REQ-G003-001:** The home route shall present every installed topic pack once as a compact summary with its title, summary, objectives, test progress, lesson progress, overall topic average, and prominent optional review status. The encompassing topic-grid sheet shall visibly introduce its installed packs' unique level range or ranges once as `Level:` or `Levels:` rather than repeating the same metadata on every card. Packs registered together in one catalog-owned group shall render as one labeled group section inside that sheet, in authored group and pack order, with the group title as a real heading.
- **REQ-G003-002:** The home route shall not render the full test sequence or lesson teaching content for any topic pack in its catalog. The explicitly opened grammar reference in REQ-G003-016/017 may show compact existing lesson summaries and examples.
- **REQ-G003-003:** The home route shall provide one prominent continue-learning action that resumes the most recently updated valid saved session or, when no session exists, starts the first test that has not yet been attempted.
- **REQ-G003-004:** Selecting a topic pack shall open `/topics/:topicId`, where the pack's objectives and every authored test appear in order.
- **REQ-G003-005:** The topic route shall preserve separate, directly accessible **Learn first** and test actions, visible **Focused tests** and **Reviews** sections without repeated classification badges, stage and skill guidance, topic-scoped mistakes, and optional review actions.
- **REQ-G003-006:** An unknown topic ID shall produce a recoverable page-level error with a route back to the topic catalog.
- **REQ-G003-007:** Adding another valid catalog entry shall add one topic summary to home without expanding that pack's tests or lessons on home.
- **REQ-G003-008:** Existing lesson, study, mistake, review, report, content-version, and learner-persistence contracts shall remain unchanged.

## Quality requirements

- **REQ-G003-009:** Home and topic navigation shall use semantic landmarks and links, visible focus, non-color progress labels, and remain usable from 320 pixels upward.
- **REQ-G003-010:** Secondary routes shall remain lazy-loaded and route metadata and path construction shall remain centralized in typed configuration.
- **REQ-G003-011:** Every home-catalog topic group shall be independently collapsible and initially collapsed each time the catalog route opens. Its complete header shall be a native keyboard-operable disclosure with the group title as a real heading and a right-aligned chevron that distinguishes closed and expanded states. Reuse the topic map's Focused tests heading typography with the existing body font size used by grammar-reference lesson names: body font, bold weight, primary text ink, authored casing, and normal letter spacing. Expanding a group shall reveal its existing cards in authored order; collapsing it shall hide those cards and their controls from reading and keyboard navigation. Expansion shall remain transient and shall not change learner data. Stats topic groups follow REQ-G003-015.

- **REQ-G003-012:** Collapsed home-catalog groups shall use the app's root-relative workbook breakpoints: four columns above 56.25rem, two columns at or below 56.25rem, and one column at or below 40.625rem. An expanded group shall span the complete grid row and retain the existing responsive three/two/one-column topic-card layout. Expansion shall first animate the header and paper to full-row width, then reveal the expanded cards through a height and opacity transition; compact layouts already spanning a row shall expand directly. Closing shall return the group to its collapsed grid placement. Repeated toggles shall cancel obsolete animation, and reduced motion shall apply the resulting layout immediately.

- **REQ-G003-013:** Closed groups shall render only their headers until their first activation loads the group's metadata. Activation shall load only that group's packs, provide accessible pending/failure feedback, permit retry, and cache successful metadata for the app session. Initial exact totals, Continue learning, progress alignment, and the Stats catalog shall remain available from the compact startup index governed by client G009.

- **REQ-G003-014:** After a group finishes opening, the catalog shall scroll only when needed to bring it into view. A group that fits the available viewport shall be shown completely; a taller group shall align its header below any sticky app header with the existing spacing token. Scrolling shall be smooth with motion enabled and immediate with reduced motion, preserve the focused control, and never run for failed, canceled, or obsolete openings.

- **REQ-G003-015:** The Stats overview topic groups shall share the home catalog's initially collapsed native disclosures and heading typography, right-aligned chevrons, independent keyboard controls, four/two/one-column collapsed layout, full-row expansion animation, cancellation, reduced motion, and viewport alignment. Open groups shall retain the existing Stats metrics, authored pack order, and View stats links. Expansion shall use the available startup summaries without requesting pack manifests or lesson/test fragments. Cumulative statistics, local backup/restore, and Drive controls shall remain directly visible and unchanged. Expansion shall reset on each Stats overview visit without changing learner data.

- **REQ-G003-016:** Each Notebook topic-group header shall offer a compact, independently operable unframed book-icon **Grammar reference** control with a group-specific accessible name, hover title, and visible keyboard focus beside its chevron. Its icon shall match the chevron’s size, ink, and stroke without a paper background, border, shadow, or visible text, with the icon pair closely spaced near the right edge to reserve width for the title, reflowing beneath the title when the header is too narrow. It shall open the existing ruled-paper modal without toggling the disclosure or navigating away. The modal shall show every lesson once, grouped by the existing pack titles in authored pack and lesson order. Each lesson shall reuse the existing worked-example paper container and display its associated Focused-test title and authored grammar summary, with all worked examples governed by REQ-G003-018. The reference shall load only the selected group's validated manifests and lessons on demand, offer accessible loading and retry feedback with a right-aligned Try again action in the shared modal footer, and never fetch scored tests or alter learner progress, completions, notes, sessions, scores, or history. Closing shall release its displayed content, invalidate obsolete load results, and use the existing purpose-labelled ×, Escape, backdrop, and trigger-focus-return behavior. Both header icons shall stay within their paper container when collapsed or expanded, including wide screens. Content and controls shall remain readable from 320 pixels upward, with enlarged text, Day/Night appearance, and reduced motion. Stats disclosures remain unchanged.

- **REQ-G003-017:** Every Notebook topic-pack card shall offer the same unframed Grammar reference book icon at the reading end of its title row. On narrow cards, the icon may occupy its own right-aligned row so the title can use the full width without shrinking text. Activating it shall open the existing modal with only that pack’s authored lesson references, summaries, and all translated worked examples, in lesson order. The pack title shall appear once as the modal heading, with correctly nested lesson headings. The icon shall have a pack-specific accessible name, hover title, visible focus, and keyboard operation, remain inside its card with enlarged text, and leave navigation, group expansion, and learner state unchanged. Pack and group references shall share loading, validation, bounded caching, retry, dismissal, and focus-return behavior. Their rendered heading IDs shall be unique across reference scopes. Stats remains unchanged.

- **REQ-G003-018:** In both group and pack references, each lesson shall display the existing associated Focused-test heading used at the top of Learn first, rather than its Preparation lesson title. Use the first authored Focused test that references the lesson; fail the reference if no such heading exists, without inventing a name. Keep the lesson name and authored grammar summary visible, on the same paper background as the existing Notebook topic-group container. Include every authored Finnish example and English translation in lesson/example order, omitting the numbered formation explanations inside an independently operable Worked examples disclosure, initially collapsed on every modal opening. Use compact example cards with tighter vertical padding and gaps, no extra paragraph margins, and inline Finnish/English text where it fits, wrapping naturally while preserving the existing fonts, colors, authored text, and disclosure behavior. Reuse the landing-page/Stats disclosure behavior and styling, including the same body-sized lesson-name heading size, authored casing, normal letter spacing, bold body font and primary text ink: native summary and details, right-aligned rotating chevron, keyboard activation, visible inset focus, deferred example rendering, cancellable geometry/opacity animation, immediate reduced motion, and responsive full-width expansion. Adapt the shared viewport alignment to the modal’s scroll area so opening examples cannot scroll the page behind it. Closing the modal shall cancel pending animation and reset every example disclosure without changing learner data or fetching test fragments.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given an expanded Notebook group, when a pack card’s book icon is activated, then the same modal shows only that pack’s authored lessons in order, with one pack heading, and closing returns focus to that icon.
- Given a failed grammar-reference load, then Try again appears at the right of the shared modal action footer and retries the selected scope.

- Given a closed or open Notebook group, when Grammar is activated, then the reference opens without changing disclosure state and shows the group’s existing pack and lesson order, summaries, and collapsed sections containing all translated worked examples.
- Given a pending or failed reference load, when the modal closes or retry is chosen, then obsolete results cannot replace the active reference, failed content is not presented, and learner data remains unchanged.
- Given one installed pack, when its home-catalog group is expanded, then one topic card and no test cards or lesson bodies are visible.
- Given multiple installed packs, when their home-catalog groups are expanded, then one topic card per pack is visible and each opens only its own topic route.
- Given home opens, when no group has been expanded, then every group heading and right-aligned chevron is visible and all topic cards are hidden.
- Given a collapsed group, when its header is clicked or activated with Enter or Space, then only that group's expansion state changes and its chevron reflects the resulting state.
- Given an expanded group, when home is reloaded or revisited after navigating away, then every group starts collapsed again.
- Given four collapsed groups on a wide workbook, when any group is opened, then it grows to full-row width before revealing its topic cards; the other groups keep their authored order.
- Given a medium or compact workbook, when groups are collapsed, then they follow the existing two/one-column breakpoints; an expanded group always spans the full row.
- Given reduced motion, when a group is toggled, then its expanded or collapsed layout appears immediately without animation.
- Given a saved test session, when home opens, then the continue-learning action resumes that session.
- Given no saved session and an unattempted test, when home opens, then the continue-learning action starts the first unattempted test in catalog and authored order.
- Given Stats opens, when groups are toggled or revisited, then they follow the home disclosure behavior while retaining Stats cards, requesting no pack content, and leaving cumulative statistics and data controls directly visible.
- Given a valid topic ID, when its topic page opens, then all tests appear in authored order with separate lesson and study actions.
- Given an invalid topic ID, when its topic page opens, then a recoverable error and topic-catalog link are visible.
