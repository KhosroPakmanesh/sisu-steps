# G005 requirements

## World and shell requirements

- **REQ-G005-001:** Every learner-facing route shall appear within one coherent interactive Nordic workbook world while keeping teaching content and the next useful action more prominent than decoration.
- **REQ-G005-002:** The shell shall suggest a softly lit school desk, bound workbook, layered page stack, and restrained depth without introducing runtime asset downloads. The desk lamp shall appear unlit in Day, radiate a warm pool of light in Night, and follow the effective device theme in Automatic. Its complete visible shade-and-arm silhouette shall form one hoverable, visibly focusable native-button target that toggles the same persisted Day/Night appearance preference as the header control by pointer or keyboard. Above 1600 pixels the lamp shall remain above the folder; at 1600 pixels and below it shall fade and move behind the complete folder so its overlap never covers the tabs or paper, while any exposed visible portion remains interactive. The remaining desk objects stay decorative. The folder shall retain breathing room from the viewport's top and side edges, including when width-constrained, and shall extend beyond the paper with the same fixed, balanced visible navy margin on both sides and the same clear space above the paper at every supported viewport width; neither folder nor paper shall appear pressed against its containing edges. Decorative pencil, ruler, and paperclip objects shall sit toward the exposed outer desk so most of each remains visible beside the folder at wide widths, while compact layouts may reduce or hide desk objects to protect content. The full-height translucent page-holding clip shall use a clearly visible double-width outer rail and inner grip with readable edge contrast, highlights, and depth, remain aligned with the paper edge, and fit within the folder at every supported width. Every routed paper shall preserve a small additional content inset after its punched left margin and reserve a right-side content-safe area wider than the clip at every supported viewport width, so content neither crowds the binding nor appears beneath the clip. Its bottom-right paper corner shall remain flat rather than displaying a decorative fold beneath the clip. Under text enlargement, decorative margins, binding space and clip geometry may shrink together when needed to preserve readable content; the default-scale shell geometry shall remain unchanged.
- **REQ-G005-003:** The header Appearance control shall remain right-aligned opposite the brand in the same header row from 320 pixels upward at the default text scale; when enlarged text no longer fits, the Appearance group shall move intact onto a following row without overlap or reduced text size. It shall present accessible Day, Automatic, and Night icons in that left-to-right order around one compact, unframed side-view mechanical toggle whose metal lever tilts left for Day, remains upright for Automatic, and tilts right for Night. It shall retain an accessible group name, native radio behavior, and the existing automatic-device, explicit-light, explicit-dark, and local-memory behavior.
- **REQ-G005-004:** Primary navigation shall use three visibly labelled native links presented as elongated blue, white, and yellow protruding subject dividers on the folder's left edge, in Topics, Reports, and Data & backup order, without numeric prefixes. Each tab shall remain fully visible within the folder's exposed left edge without viewport or folder clipping, while its right edge tucks beneath the paper. The stack shall begin with visible breathing room below the paper top, and the active route shall use a shaded tab surface rather than a selected border. The stack shall respond to hover and keyboard focus and remain sticky within the folder after scrolling past the paper top. It shall stop at the folder boundary, preserve active-page semantics, and never delay route availability.
- **REQ-G005-005:** Every back-navigation link, including Reports and Data & backup, shall resemble the same folded page corner or attached bookmark while retaining an explicit visible destination label and native link behavior.

## Object-control requirements

- **REQ-G005-006:** Primary, secondary, review, compact, text, file, dialog, and destructive actions shall share one recognisable cut-paper construction. Each action button or action-link shall use its label-driven intrinsic width instead of stretching across its containing surface, while respecting the available width and retaining its practical touch height. Back-navigation and recovery actions shall stay at the reading start; topic-cover, continue-assignment, and result decision groups shall be centered; forward lesson actions, test-card actions, exercise submission/reveal/continuation groups, note actions, archive/clear actions, and confirmation actions shall align to the reading end. Related actions shall remain one compact wrapping group rather than being spread across unused space. Hierarchy and state shall vary through readable ink, edge accents, internal marks, and explicit labels rather than a mixture of conventional buttons, stamps, cards, bookmarks, or slips, while native button/link semantics remain unchanged.
- **REQ-G005-007:** Radio choices shall place pencil-drawn circles on perforated answer slips, checkboxes shall resemble marked notebook boxes, text fields shall resemble true answer lines, and selects shall resemble tabbed card selectors rather than browser-form fields while retaining visible state, labels, keyboard use, touch targets, and assistive-technology semantics.
- **REQ-G005-008:** Word-order controls shall resemble physical vocabulary cutouts that lift and settle when activated; clicking and keyboard operation shall remain complete, and dragging shall never be required.
- **REQ-G005-009:** Backup and restore controls shall use the shared cut-paper action construction with archive-appropriate labels and accents while preserving accepted file types, action labels, status text, and deliberate restore behavior.
- **REQ-G005-010:** Test, lesson, and topic progress shall resemble printed rulers without a pencil marker, and completion, score, correct, incorrect, skipped, warning, and danger states shall retain explicit text or symbols in addition to physical styling and color.
- **REQ-G005-011:** Focus shall be strongly visible as a pencil or ink outline on every surface, and tooltips or margin notes shall contain only supplemental information that is also available through accessible text when needed.

## Page-scene requirements

- **REQ-G005-012:** The catalog shall present topics as distinct compact bound exercise-book covers with printed cover fields rather than dashboard cards. The topic grid shall show three equal-width covers per row above 900 pixels, two from 651 through 900 pixels, and one at 650 pixels and below. Each landing cover shall retain its title, short summary, tests-tried and lessons-read progress, optional due-review action, and topic action; detailed statistics and objectives shall remain available on the topic route instead of lengthening the catalog cover. Topic covers and the continue-learning assignment shall share one warm amber paper palette while remaining distinct through their cover and clipped-slip construction. A catalog cover shall show no left-edge inset or spine in either its resting or hovered state, leaving the lifted paper depth as its only emphasized edge feedback. The continue-learning assignment and topic progress summary shall share the same warm, taped, ruled-paper assignment construction while retaining their respective action content and four-value information layout: 2×2 above 560 pixels and one column at 560 pixels and below, retaining the existing compact reflow under `REQ-G005-019`. The topic route shall present an open fold-out study map whose warm goals sheet retains its established grid-paper pattern, tape, clipped silhouette, spacing, and typography while lifting on hover, and whose group dividers and connected, punched index cards share one construction and never imply unavailable or locked content.
- **REQ-G005-013:** Lessons shall retain their current available-width teaching sequence whose lesson navigation, focus contract, key points, worked examples, vocabulary, optional practice, annotations, tabs, and foldouts use recognisable school-paper objects and become a readable single-page sequence on narrow screens.
- **REQ-G005-014:** Study shall resemble an isolated exercise sheet whose pinned target note, perforated answer slips, ruled answer line, vocabulary cutouts, sentence-building strip and pocket, progress ruler, teacher feedback slip, and fold-out sentence explanation remain easy to operate; results shall resemble a returned paper with a score mark and plainly labelled next-action slips.
- **REQ-G005-015:** Reports shall resemble a Finnish school ledger whose totals, topic dividers, charts, mastery marks, and correction prompt belong to the ledger rather than generic dashboard cards, and data management shall resemble an archive drawer whose backup, restore, reset, history materials, and individual clearing rows read as labelled file materials.

## System-state and quality requirements

- **REQ-G005-016:** Loading shall resemble a pencil sketch in progress on a loose drafting sheet, notices shall resemble attached notes, errors shall resemble correction slips, and empty states shall remain plain-language sheets with an obvious next action; none shall fall back to a generic rounded web card.
- **REQ-G005-017:** Destructive confirmation shall use a modal loose-sheet dialog that repeats the specific consequence, defaults focus to a safe cancellation action, cancels on Escape, returns focus to the initiating control, and performs work only after explicit confirmation.
- **REQ-G005-018:** At reduced motion, route, paper, stamp, pencil, token, light, and parallax motion shall become immediate static states; at all preferences, navigation and content shall not wait for animation.
- **REQ-G005-019:** From 320 pixels upward, the world shall become a readable pocket-notebook composition without horizontal page scrolling, clipped labels, inaccessible controls, or loss of WCAG 2.2 AA contrast; it shall preserve semantic landmarks, local-only operation, practical touch targets, and existing learning and data behavior.

## Stationery-refinement requirements

- **REQ-G005-020:** Correct, incorrect, skipped, lesson-complete, and session-complete state changes shall receive a brief readable teacher-stamp response that never delays feedback and becomes static under reduced motion.
- **REQ-G005-022:** Before submission, each applicable answer type shall provide clearly labelled correction or eraser actions for clearing the current choice or typed draft and for undoing or clearing the current word-order draft. These actions shall not alter stored attempts, mistakes, scores, or history.
- **REQ-G005-023:** The private topic and lesson notes required by `REQ-G001-102`–`106` shall resemble attached sticky notes while retaining a visible label, native textarea, character count, save/removal wording, keyboard access, and status text. The topic note shall appear after the complete lessons-and-tests learning map and shall lift on hover or focus-within without changing its form semantics or stored-note behavior.
- **REQ-G005-025:** Active subject tabs, selected lesson tabs, the continue-learning assignment, the catalog statistics record strip, the topic goals sheet, sticky note and group-divider sheets, and interactive topic or test cards shall move forward or lift briefly on active, selected, hover, or focus-within states without changing navigation order or requiring pointer hover. The continue-learning assignment, topic goals sheet, topic sticky note, topic group dividers, and catalog topic covers shall use the same lift distance; the informational record strip may use a smaller hover-only lift without implying that it is clickable.
- **REQ-G005-026:** Typed learner input shall remain visibly aligned to a ruled answer line and may receive restrained graphite feedback while non-empty without using handwriting for the learner's text.
- **REQ-G005-027:** The stationery refinement shall introduce no accordion, disclosure, collapsible panel, expandable tool tray, or other collapse/expand behavior.

## Consistency refinement requirements

- **REQ-G005-028:** Worked-example explanations shall use the same body-font size and line height as lesson prose: 1rem and 1.65, equivalent to 16 pixels and 26.4 pixels at the default browser text scale. Existing body and display font families shall remain unchanged.
- **REQ-G005-029:** Essential labels and metadata, including folder navigation, shall use at least 0.75rem text at the default browser text scale. Their containers shall accommodate wrapping and text enlargement without clipping required wording. Decorative, assistive-technology-hidden stamps are excluded.
- **REQ-G005-030:** Optional Practice and scored Study answer slips shall share 1rem padding and a 0.5rem radio-to-text gap above 560 pixels, and 0.75rem padding with the same gap at 560 pixels and below. This geometry shall not change their separate learning or scoring behavior.
- **REQ-G005-031:** Lesson paragraphs and worked explanations shall retain their current available-width behavior without a new maximum reading-width cap. The learner accepted longer desktop reading lines; narrow-screen clipping and text-enlargement failures remain defects under `REQ-G005-019`.

- **REQ-G005-032:** At viewport widths from 320 pixels upward and root text sizes through 200% of the default, the header, primary navigation and routed paper shall reflow without document overflow or clipped required text and controls. Content layout thresholds shall adapt to the requested text size; pixel thresholds elsewhere describe the default scale. Appearance choices shall retain their native order and keyboard behavior. Decorative spacing may contract when enlarged text needs more room, while body text continues to follow the requested size and default-scale geometry remains unchanged.

## Withdrawn refinement requirements

- **REQ-G005-021 (withdrawn 2026-08-23):** The proposed Finnish-character pencil-case toolbar was removed from scope at the learner's request. Typed answers continue to use the native text field and keyboard.
- **REQ-G005-024 (withdrawn 2026-08-26):** The paper-clipped **Show studied tests only** report filter was removed at the learner's request. Reports now show every test row.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given any learner-facing route, when it opens in Day or Night appearance, then it clearly belongs to the same physical workbook world and its required content remains easier to find than its decoration.
- Given the Appearance control, when it appears in the header, then it reads as one compact side-view physical toggle with a metal mounting base and lever, has no enclosing colored panel or border, and uses icons instead of visible Day/Automatic/Night words.
- Given the Appearance control, when a learner chooses Day, Automatic, or Night by pointer or keyboard, then the lever tilts left, remains upright, or tilts right respectively and the existing light, automatic, or dark preference behavior is applied and remembered with a visibly emphasized icon.
- Given the workbook shell at any supported width, when it appears, then Topics, Reports, and Data & backup are elongated blue, white, and yellow native links on the folder's left edge with their right edges beneath the paper, the active link is distinguished by a darker shade rather than a selected border, the green decorative tab is absent, and the Day/Automatic/Night switch remains in the header without redundant Appearance or Desk light headings.
- Given the folder navigation near the paper top, when the learner hovers or keyboard-focuses a tab, then it visibly lifts without changing link semantics; when scrolling passes the paper top, the tab stack follows the viewport and stops within the folder instead of floating beyond it.
- Given keyboard or assistive-technology use, when a learner operates navigation, actions, fields, choices, tokens, file restore, or a select, then native semantics, visible focus, labels, and state remain complete despite the custom appearance.
- Given any action button or action-link, when it appears in the shell content, a learning route, data management, results, or a confirmation sheet, then it uses the same cut-paper construction, occupies only its label-driven intrinsic width up to the available space, retains its practical touch height, follows the role-based start/center/end placement contract, remains grouped compactly with related actions, and remains distinguishable by its visible label, ink, edge accent, and state.
- Given repeated informational surfaces on the catalog, topic, lesson, study, reports, or data route, when they appear together, then they use the documented exercise-book, index-card, answer-slip, ledger, or archive family for that route instead of unrelated generic rounded cards or chips.
- Given route navigation, when a page changes, then content is immediately available and any visual page-turn effect is brief, non-blocking, and absent under reduced motion.
- Given a lesson at a wide viewport, when it opens, then it retains its current available-width reading order without a new prose-width cap; at 320 pixels, the same content follows a clear single-column reading order without horizontal scrolling.
- Given a destructive data action, when it is selected, then a consequence-specific confirmation sheet opens with Cancel focused; Escape or Cancel closes it without work and returns focus, while the explicit confirm action alone performs the existing operation.
- Given a learner who cannot perceive color or metaphor, when any progress, answer, feedback, score, warning, or destructive state appears, then its meaning remains available through visible words, symbols, borders, or structure.
- Given reduced motion, when the learner navigates and interacts, then all content and state changes remain immediate and the desk, paper, stamp, pencil, and token animations are suppressed.
- Given the catalog, topic, lesson, study, results, reports, and data routes at 320, 768, and 1440 pixels, then each has its approved scene identity without overlap, clipping, or horizontal page scrolling.
- Given any G005 refinement, when the existing learning and persistence suites run, then routes, content, grading, sessions, report values, and all pre-existing learner-data behavior remain unchanged while note storage follows `REQ-G001-102`–`106`.
- Given answer feedback, lesson completion, or session completion, when the state changes, then a teacher stamp appears immediately with the complete text feedback still present and no animation under reduced motion.
- Given an unsubmitted answer, when the learner uses its correction or eraser action, then only the current choice, typed draft, or word-order draft changes and persisted learner history is untouched.
- Given a topic or lesson note, when it is displayed, edited, saved, removed, or reports a failure, then its native field, scope, character limit, explicit action, draft, and status remain understandable without recognizing the sticky-note metaphor.
- Given the catalog, when the continue-learning assignment and topic covers appear, then they use the same warm amber paper palette while remaining visually distinct, and hovering or focusing within either surface produces the same restrained lift.
- Given the catalog statistics record strip, when a pointer hovers over it, then the whole strip lifts slightly with stronger paper depth without changing its values, gaining a click action, or moving under reduced motion.
- Given the continue-learning assignment and topic progress summary, when both are inspected, then they use the same warm ruled paper, dashed border, red left edge, tape strip, folded corner, and clipped assignment silhouette while the topic summary preserves its four values, its 2×2 wider layout, and its existing one-column compact reflow; when the summary is hovered, it lifts without gaining click semantics, its labels remain strongly contrasted against the paper, and its values use the same muted secondary ink as the continue-learning topic subtitle.
- Given the topic header, when the folded **All topics** link precedes the level/test eyebrow, then a full spacing-scale step clearly separates the two without changing their wording, order, or semantics.
- Given the **Focused tests** and **Reviews** group dividers, when either is hovered, then the complete divider sheet lifts by the shared distance without gaining click semantics, a pointer cursor, or motion under reduced-motion preference.
- Given the topic goals sheet, when it appears or is hovered, then its base paper uses the warm landing-page palette and lifts by the shared distance while its existing grid pattern, tape, clipped silhouette, spacing, typography, objectives, and informational semantics remain unchanged.
- Given a topic note, when the topic route appears, then the note follows the complete lessons-and-tests learning map with only its normal adjacent margin and lifts on hover or focus-within while its field, actions, draft, saved value, status, and local persistence behavior remain unchanged.
- Given any stationery-refinement screen, when it is used at a supported width, then no stationery tool or teaching section opens or closes through a disclosure interaction.

- Given worked examples beside or after teaching prose, then both use the approved body-text role while headings and font families retain their existing hierarchy.
- Given essential labels at the default text scale, then each is at least 12 pixels and remains fully readable after responsive reflow and user text-spacing overrides.
- Given the same answer choice in optional Practice and scored Study, then its responsive padding and radio/text gap match without changing recorded progress.
- Given a compact destructive action, then size styling cannot replace its danger ink and paper palette in either appearance or any applicable interaction state.
- Given no unresolved mistakes or no review currently due, then the study route shows an informative empty sheet and a return action, without presenting the absence of work as an operation error.

- Given a 320-pixel viewport, when text is enlarged to 150% or 200%, then the header and notebook adapt without hiding required content, shrinking the requested text, or losing navigation/Appearance controls; returning to the default text scale restores the existing geometry.
