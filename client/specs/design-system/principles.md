# Design-system principles

Sisu Steps should feel calm, encouraging, precise, and private during repeated Finnish study. Teaching clarity is more important than decorative novelty.

## Principles

- Keep study workflows readable and scannable at desktop and narrow mobile widths.
- Make lessons, scored tests, optional practice, skipped answers, corrections, and delayed mastery visibly distinct.
- Make destructive actions and local download/restore actions explicit.
- Communicate correctness, status, and risk with text or iconography in addition to color.
- Use consistent spacing, typography, borders, radius, focus, control, elevation, and breakpoint tokens.
- Reuse established components and interaction patterns before inventing a variant.
- Keep learner input and error messages visible and recoverable when an operation fails.

## Product feel

- Quiet, warm, and uncluttered.
- Beginner-friendly without looking childish.
- Trustworthy and focused on learning.
- Local and under the learner's control.
