About your data

Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Sisu Steps is a free Finnish exercise book maintained by Khosro Pakmanesh. You can study without creating an account or connecting Google Drive. This page explains what the app stores and what happens if you choose to use its optional backup features.

On this device

The app saves answers, attempts, unfinished sessions, mistakes, lesson completions, progress, and private notes in your browser's IndexedDB. It also remembers your appearance choice and, if you make a Drive checkpoint, the last successful backup date in browser storage. These records stay in that browser unless you explicitly export a backup or use a Google Drive action. Sisu Steps has no account or server that stores your learner progress.

Optional Google Drive checkpoint

Google Drive is contacted only when you choose a Drive action. You select a Google account through Google's authorization window. Sisu Steps requests only permission to manage its own hidden application-data folder (drive.appdata); it does not request or read your Google name, email address, profile picture, or other Drive files. Access tokens stay in memory, not in browser storage or exported backups.

When you choose Back up to Google Drive, the app sends a versioned snapshot of your learner data to that account's hidden app storage. The snapshot includes answers, progress, unfinished work, and private notes. Backup is manual: there is no automatic sync at startup or when you close the page. Sisu Steps adds no separate password or custom end-to-end encryption to this snapshot. Google's storage and account protections apply.

The app uses Google data only to inspect, create, restore, or delete your requested checkpoint. It does not sell that data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Copies, deletion, and retention

Download backup creates a JSON copy on your device under your control. Clear all history removes local learner progress and notes, but does not delete a Drive checkpoint or a downloaded file. Delete Drive backup removes the app's current Drive checkpoint without clearing local data. Disconnect Google Drive ends the current authorization; it does not delete either copy. Clearing browser-site data may also remove local progress and preferences.

Sisu Steps does not set a retention period for copies kept in your browser, downloaded files, or your Google Drive. You manage those copies through the controls above and your device or Google account. Google may remove hidden app data if you remove the app from your Drive account. A downloaded JSON file is not protected by a Sisu Steps password, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Site and provider services

GitHub Pages serves the website and its lesson files. If you use Drive backup, Google's identity and Drive services process that operation. Those providers may process technical request data under their own privacy notices. Sisu Steps does not send your learner records to its maintainer as part of normal study or a Drive action.

Questions and issues

For privacy or other enquiries, email khosro.pakmanesh@gmail.com. If you email, your address and message are used to respond to your enquiry. To report a bug, open a GitHub issue. GitHub issues are public, so do not include private notes, answers, backups, or other personal information in a report.
