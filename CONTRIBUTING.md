# Contributing to Sisu Steps

Thank you for helping make Sisu Steps better. Finnish corrections, clearer explanations, new exercises, accessibility improvements, tests, documentation, design, and code are all welcome. Small corrections are just as useful as large contributions.

## Choose the easiest way

- **Found a mistake or have an idea?** Open an issue. You do not need to prepare code or accept the contributor agreement for an idea, bug report, or brief factual correction.
- **Want to make a change?** Fork or clone the repository and open a pull request.
- **Want to contribute regularly?** Contact the Maintainer. Trusted contributors can be invited to work directly in the official repository, including through a direct workflow agreed with the Maintainer.

For a significant change, a short discussion first can save everyone time. Otherwise, choose a focused improvement, follow the repository guidance, run the relevant checks when possible, and explain what you changed.

If an issue contains substantial original code, exercises, explanations, or other copyrightable material that the Project would like to use, the Maintainer will ask its author to accept the contributor agreement before incorporating it.

## One-time contributor agreement

You accept the contributor agreement once, before your first Contribution. For a pull request, select the checkbox in the pull-request template. A direct collaborator can accept through another written record agreed with the Maintainer. No repeated acceptance or link to an earlier acceptance is required.

In plain language:

- you have the right to contribute the work;
- you keep copyright in your contribution;
- Sisu Steps may merge, edit, and use it as part of this free, noncommercial project;
- neither the Maintainer nor anyone else may turn the Project or your contribution into a commercial product under this License; and
- you can stop contributing whenever you want, while contributions already made remain part of the Project under the agreed terms.

Read [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) for the complete terms.

## Finnish-learning content

Please identify the source behind a linguistic correction or new claim when possible. Do not copy protected exercises or substantial text from another course, book, website, or project. Mention AI assistance so the content can receive extra language and teaching review.

If you are unsure about anything, open an issue or contact the Maintainer. The goal is to help good contributions happen—not to make the process difficult.
