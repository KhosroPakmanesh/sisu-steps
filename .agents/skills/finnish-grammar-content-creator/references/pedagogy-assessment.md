# Pedagogy assessment record

Use this template for the actual pre-authoring and final decisions. The [pre-authoring rubric](../../../../specs/content-authoring.md#2-pre-authoring-finnish-teaching-pedagogy-assessment), [final rubric](../../../../specs/content-authoring.md#5-final-finnish-teaching-pedagogy-assessment), and [record requirements](../../../../specs/content-authoring.md#saved-assessment-record) live in the repository policy.

Read the relevant rubric at each gate. Complete the pre-authoring assessment before bulk question writing and the final assessment on finished, rendered content. Record evidence for every applicable criterion and explain scoped exceptions. Use the policy's dispositions and blocking rules; a structural validator cannot approve Finnish teaching quality.

## Record template

Save the record at `specs/content-assessments/<pack-id>.md`. Replace the prompts below with actual decisions and evidence; leave unperformed work explicitly unverified.

```markdown
# Pack pedagogy assessment

## Topic, level, and scope

State the pack ID, topic boundary, starting and expected ending level,
user constraints, exclusions, and the affected lesson/exercise inventory.

## Sources

List authoritative grammar and usage sources and the decisions they support.

## Coverage blueprint

Map important skills, prerequisites, expected misconceptions, vocabulary,
number scopes, and sequence. Record proposed per-test and total counts,
shared preparation relationships, response-format variation, and Review scope.

## Pre-authoring assessment

Disposition:
Evidence against each applicable pre-authoring criterion:
Findings, impact, resolution, and limitations:
Actual date and content state assessed:

## Final assessment

Disposition:
Final lesson, test, scored, and optional-practice counts:
Evidence against each applicable final criterion:
Complete affected inventories, exceptions, and inflection judgments:
Vocabulary category/type counts and prerequisite provenance:
Question recall-list comparisons, missing/unnecessary entries, and corrections:
Rendered prompt/feedback, grading-alternative, and mastery-pair evidence:
Findings, impact, resolution, and remaining limitations:
Actual date and content state assessed:

## Technical validation

Record commands and results, separate automated from rendered/manual checks,
and name anything failed, unavailable, or not performed.

## Compatibility and final decision

Record affected versions, approved history consequences, and preserved scope.
Give the actual final decision and remaining limitations.
```

For an audit or narrow correction, identify the exact affected class and the evidence inherited from an earlier record. Preserve dated earlier findings and do not fabricate a retrospective pre-authoring approval.
